## Description

Rostami Group LLC develops, owns, and operates experiences, media
properties, and development programs designed to bring people together
and create lasting value. The company begins with RallyFest, a
festival-style sports experience, and is built to expand across live
events, original media and storytelling, youth sports, education, and
other community-centered ventures. Through disciplined execution,
creative ambition, and a long-term approach, Rostami Group builds brands
and programs that people anticipate, trust, and return to year after
year.

# Brand Architecture

Rostami Group LLC is the only legal company. Individual offerings may
use distinct customer-facing product or trade names, but they remain
owned and operated by Rostami Group LLC unless a future legal, risk,
investment, or operational need justifies forming another entity.

# Initial Strategic Focus

1.  Validate RallyFest through a focused pilot that proves participant
    demand, registration performance, operational feasibility, and
    sponsor value.
2.  Build the systems, brand standards, partnerships, media
    capabilities, and operating discipline required to make RallyFest a
    recurring annual property.
3.  Use media first to strengthen RallyFest through storytelling,
    content, livestreaming, marketing, and year-round audience
    engagement.
4.  Explore additional events or development programs only after the
    flagship concept demonstrates clear demand and execution capability.
5.  Keep all activities within Rostami Group LLC initially, while
    tracking each business line separately and reassessing legal
    separation when risk, ownership, scale, or investment requires it.
